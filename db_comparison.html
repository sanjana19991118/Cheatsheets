RDBMS vs. NoSQL (Non-RDBMS)

Understanding the difference between structured and unstructured data storage is critical for architecture design, as seen in cloud services like AWS.

1. The Visual Difference

RDBMS (Table/Rows)
[ID | Name | Age]
[1 | Alice| 30 ]
[2 | Bob | 25 ]

NoSQL (Document/Key-Value)
{ "id": 1, "name": "Alice", "meta": {...} }
{ "id": 2, "name": "Bob", "hobbies": [...] }

3. When to use which?

  • Choose RDBMS (e.g., RDS) if:
    • Your data is highly structured and relationships are clear.
    • Data integrity and ACID compliance (transactions) are non-negotiable (e.g., Banking).
    • You don't expect massive, unpredictable spikes in data volume.
  • Choose NoSQL (e.g., DynamoDB) if:
    • You have unstructured or rapidly changing data.
    • You need massive scale and high throughput (e.g., Social media feeds, IoT).
    • You require rapid development cycles with changing schemas.
